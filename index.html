About FinNews AI

FinNews AI combines real-time news retrieval, generative AI, large language models, and natural language simplification, built on FastAPI and modern web technologies.

Our goal is accessibility and education — helping anyone understand what financial headlines mean. It is not investment advice, and it never tells you to buy, sell, or hold anything.
